Sliding block puzzle: slide, unblock and escape. Free hints, no signup.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Block Escape is a classic sliding block puzzle in the spirit of Rush Hour. One red block, one exit, and a board full of other blocks in the way. Slide them aside, clear the path, and escape!

Every level is verified solvable by a built-in solver, so you will never get stuck on an impossible board. Puzzles start gentle and ramp up into genuine brain teasers that reward planning ahead.

HOW IT WORKS
- Drag any block along its length: horizontal blocks slide left and right, vertical blocks slide up and down
- Clear a path for the red block to reach the glowing exit
- Fewer moves is better. Every level shows the best possible move count, so you always know what to aim for

FEATURES
- 10 handcrafted unblock puzzles with a smooth difficulty curve
- Best-possible move count shown for every level
- Free, unlimited hints whenever you are stuck
- Undo and Restart at any time
- Progress saved automatically on your device
- Works offline, no account or sign-up needed
- Clean wooden-board look, no clutter, no timers, no pressure
- A quick how-to-play guide on first launch

WHO IT IS FOR
Fans of sliding puzzles, unblock games, logic puzzles and brain teasers. Perfect for a quick break or a longer session, and easy to pick up whether you are new to the genre or a puzzle veteran.

Block Escape is free to play and supported by banner ads. More levels are planned.
