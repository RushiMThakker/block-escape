SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max):
Relaxing sliding block puzzle: 25 levels, Advent calendar, free hints.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Take a calm, satisfying break with Block Escape, a classic sliding block puzzle in the spirit of Rush Hour. One red block, one exit, and a board full of other blocks in the way. Slide them aside, clear the path, and escape!

Every level is verified solvable by a built-in solver, so you will never get stuck on an impossible board. Puzzles start gentle and grow into genuine brain teasers that reward planning ahead, so you can unwind in a couple of minutes or settle in for a longer session.

HOW IT WORKS
- Drag any block along its length: horizontal blocks slide left and right, vertical blocks slide up and down
- Clear a path for the red block to reach the glowing exit
- Fewer moves is better. Every level shows the best possible move count, so you always know what to aim for

FEATURES
- 25 unblock puzzles with a smooth difficulty curve, from a 2-move warm-up to boards that need 30 moves
- Advent calendar: from 1 to 24 December a new door opens each day with its own puzzle. Missed a day? Every door stays open to catch up until 6 January
- A festive look for the season: the red block becomes a sleigh, the others become gift boxes, with string lights and falling snow. It switches on automatically from 1 December to 6 January, and you can set it to On or Off in Settings
- Best-possible move count shown for every level, so you can chase a perfect score
- Free, unlimited hints whenever you are stuck
- Undo and Restart at any time, so you can try ideas without worry
- Progress saved automatically on your device, with each cleared level unlocking the next
- Works offline, no account or sign-up needed
- Clean wooden-board look, no clutter, no timers, no pressure
- A quick how-to-play guide on first launch

WHO IT IS FOR
Fans of sliding puzzles, unblock games, logic puzzles and brain teasers. Perfect for a quick break on the commute or a longer evening session, and easy to pick up whether you are new to the genre or a puzzle veteran.

Block Escape is free to play and supported by banner ads. More levels are planned.
