Privacy Policy for Block Escape

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Block Escape ("the app") is a single-player sliding-block puzzle game developed by Rushi Thakker. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data the app collects itself

Block Escape does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. Your level progress (which levels are cleared, your best move counts) is stored only on your own device, using Android's local app storage. This data is never transmitted anywhere, never leaves your device, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Block Escape shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage information (such as an advertising ID) to serve and measure ads, and may use this data as described in Google's Partner Sites policy. This app does not control what AdMob collects — see Google's own privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — required to fetch ads.
  • Vibrate — used for short haptic feedback on certain in-game actions (e.g. a blocked move, winning a level). No data is collected or transmitted by this permission.

Children's privacy

Block Escape does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
